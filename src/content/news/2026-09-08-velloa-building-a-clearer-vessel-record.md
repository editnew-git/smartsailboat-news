SSB Velloa’s April 2026 records offer an introduction to work on daily captain’s logging and task coordination, alongside discussions about OTTO’s development within [Smart Sail Boat](https://smartsailboat.com).

## Building the daily record

An April 8, 2026 meeting summary described progress on Velloa’s near real-time captain’s log and task-coordination workflow. The summary reported that the automation pipeline was working for daily operational logging at that time.

## Exploring OTTO’s direction

An April 16, 2026 meeting summary explored photo-based asset recognition, inventory and location tracking, and Slack access. The discussion also considered the importance of cloud-connected and offline or local operation for emergency scenarios.

These topics formed part of the development direction being discussed in April. Together, the two dated records introduce Velloa through the practical work of documenting vessel operations and considering how that record could become more useful.
