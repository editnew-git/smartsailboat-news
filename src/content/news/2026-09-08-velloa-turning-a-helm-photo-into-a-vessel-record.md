On May 7, 2026, Velloa’s captain shared a helm photo and identified the wheel. OTTO used that contribution to record the helm wheel and pedestal console in the vessel inventory.

The entry shows a concrete step in documenting Velloa: a photograph supplied visual context, the captain identified the equipment, and OTTO added the assembly to the vessel record.

The documented result was an inventory entry. The record does not establish an equipment inspection or a functional test.
